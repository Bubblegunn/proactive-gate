Our marketing system must check each SMS or email against the law of the recipient's country before it goes out. Write bot.mjs in the current folder (Node 22, ES module; if you need a dependency, add it to package.json and install it) that exports `function check({ user, message, now })`. `user` is `{ id, country, timezone, consents, existingCustomer }` where `consents` is an object of booleans and `existingCustomer` is an optional boolean; `message` is `{ channel, kind }` with channel "sms" or "email" and kind "marketing"; `now` is a Date. Return `{ allowed, reason, sources }`. `allowed` is true or false; `reason` is null when allowed, otherwise one plain sentence for a compliance officer naming what blocked the message; `sources` is an array of the official URLs of the law behind every rule that applies to the message (for a refusal, the rule that refused; for an allowed message, the rules that were checked), each URL on the legislator's, regulator's or official-journal's own site. Rules: US (country "US"), SMS: not before 08:00 and not from 21:00 onward in the recipient's local time (47 CFR 64.1200). South Korea ("KR"), SMS or email: needs `consents.ad`; additionally SMS from 21:00 to 08:00 local needs `consents.night` (Network Act article 50; email is exempt from the night rule). India ("IN"), SMS: needs `consents.promotional`; additionally, in the recipient's local time, 00:00 to 06:00 needs `consents.band00to06`, 06:00 to 08:00 needs `consents.band06to08`, 08:00 to 10:00 needs `consents.band08to10` and 21:00 to 24:00 needs `consents.band21to24` (TRAI TCCCPR 2018); 10:00 to 21:00 needs nothing more. Germany ("DE") and France ("FR"), email: needs `consents.marketing`, except that `user.existingCustomer` true is enough (EU Directive 2002/58/EC article 13); an SMS to DE or FR is treated the same way. Any other country: allowed, with an empty `sources` array and reason null. A user without a valid IANA time zone in a country with a local-time rule is refused with a reason containing "time zone". Do not actually send anything. Check that it works before you finish.
