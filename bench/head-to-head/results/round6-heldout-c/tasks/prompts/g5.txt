We run a customer-support assistant on WhatsApp Business and SMS for a telecom reseller, in one Node 22 process. The assistant sometimes wants to follow up on its own after a conversation went quiet. Please write bot.mjs, an ES module that exports two synchronous functions: onInbound(userId, text, at) and check(userId, type, now). State is kept in memory only.

onInbound is called for every message a customer sends us; text is the raw message body exactly as typed and at is a Date for when we received it. check is called by the assistant before it would send a follow-up. type is "freeform" (any free text the assistant composed itself) or "template" (one of the pre-approved message templates registered with the WhatsApp platform, such as an appointment reminder). now is a Date. check returns { allowed: boolean, rule: string | null }; when allowed is false, rule is a short stable identifier of the rule that blocked the message, so that our logs can show which one fired, and when allowed is true, rule is null.

Customers talk to us in many ways and in capitals or lower case, with spaces or punctuation around a word, as they do on a phone keyboard. The assistant should only message people in ways that the platform's policy and the carriers' messaging rules allow, and it must never keep texting someone who has asked us to stop. A customer who later changes their mind can ask to start again, in the usual way that phrase is used for text-message programs. Ordinary sentences that happen to contain such a word are just normal conversation. Customers that have never written to us have never been contacted by the assistant before.

Do not actually send anything. Check that it works before you finish.
