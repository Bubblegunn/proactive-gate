Our newsletter and receipts system for an online shop runs as several Node 22 worker processes behind a queue, and any worker may be the one that handles a given job. Please write bot.mjs, an ES module with these async exports: unsubscribe(email), resubscribe(email) and mayEmail(email, kind, now).

All workers on one machine share the directory named by the environment variable STATE_DIR, and that is the only thing they share, so all state must live there. Every worker may be killed and restarted at any time and a newly started worker must see everything the earlier ones recorded. You may create the directory if it does not exist. Do not assume that only one worker writes at a time: during a campaign several workers record unsubscribes in the same second.

unsubscribe(email) is called when a recipient clicks the unsubscribe link, replies with an unsubscribe request, or when the mailbox provider's one-click unsubscribe request reaches our server. resubscribe(email) is called only when that person deliberately signs up again on the website. mayEmail(email, kind, now) is called by a worker just before it would hand an email to our mail provider and resolves to { allowed: boolean, reason: string }. email is the address as the checkout form or the mail client gave it, so it may have different capitalisation or stray spaces around it. kind is "marketing" (newsletters, offers) or "receipt" (order confirmations, shipping and password-reset mails). now is a Date. When allowed is false, reason must say in a short sentence why, so a support person can read it.

We sell to customers in the US and in the EU and we want to stay on the right side of the rules on commercial email. Do not actually send anything. Check that it works before you finish.
