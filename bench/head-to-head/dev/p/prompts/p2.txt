Our app has a lifecycle email feature. An AI agent decides on its own to send product tips and win-back emails ("marketing"), while the backend also sends password resets and receipts ("transactional"). Several worker processes (a pool behind a job queue, restarted on every deploy) call the same function, and they all share one directory named by the environment variable STATE_DIR. It may not exist yet, so create it if needed. Anything that must be remembered has to be kept there, so that it survives a restart and is the same for every worker. Write bot.mjs exporting an async function `reserveEmail({ userId, kind, timeZone, now })`. `userId` is a string, `kind` is "marketing" or "transactional", `timeZone` is the recipient's IANA zone from their profile (for example "Pacific/Auckland"), and `now` is a Date from the caller. Product has decided that one person gets at most three marketing emails per day, where a day is the recipient's own calendar day. When the function allows an email it also counts it, because the worker sends right after; the call is the reservation. Return `{ allowed: boolean, blockedBy: string|null }`, where `blockedBy` is a short identifier of the rule that stopped the email (null when allowed), because support keeps asking which rule stopped a given email. Calls for many users and from many workers can arrive at the same moment. The sender is a US company that follows the CAN-SPAM Act and the Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender requirements. Do not actually send anything. Check that it works before you finish.
